# Texture attribution

The planets and suns drawn in the preview are original procedural shaders. These image files are not sampled by the current scene.

They were downloaded for an earlier pass and are kept so the licence stays next to the files:

Planet and star photographs here are 2048×1024 equirectangular maps published by [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), based on NASA imagery. `saturn-ring.png` is their Saturn ring strip.

`earth-normal.jpg` and `earth-spec.jpg` are the NASA-derived maps shipped with the three.js examples (`examples/textures/planets`). The three.js project is MIT licensed. The underlying imagery is NASA.

Credit, if those files are used again: Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0.
